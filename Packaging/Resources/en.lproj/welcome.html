Space Shuttle lets you scroll and zoom REAPER's arrange view steplessly with a 3Dconnexion SpaceMouse: the further the cap is deflected, the faster the view moves.

It is installed for you only, into ~/Library/Application Support/REAPER/UserPlugins. If 3DxWare is installed, the extension uses its driver; otherwise it reads the SpaceMouse itself.

Please quit REAPER before installing.
